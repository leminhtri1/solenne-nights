SOLENNE NIGHTS is an original open-world crime game in three.js: steal any car, bike or boat in the sun-soaked bay city of Solenne, outrun a five-star police response (ramming and window-firing patrol cars, roadblocks, tactical vans and the HALO-1 searchlight helicopter), and play nine story chapters against the developer Victor Kessane, from golden hour to neon night. Streets are full of traffic, pedestrians who duck and run, cafés, food carts and bus shelters; between chapters there are street races, taxi fares, deliveries, 24 Sun Tokens and engine, tyre and armour upgrades at Chromatica Customs. The HUD has a colour city minimap with the police search zone, a WANTED banner, the response roster, named weapons, a respect ladder and a full-screen map with the story so far. Everything (city, cars, people, music, sound) is generated in code and runs from static files.

Controls: WASD drive or walk, mouse look and aim, F enter or steal, Space handbrake, V camera view, M map, T jobs, E shops, Esc pause.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts   # then open http://localhost:8080
```
